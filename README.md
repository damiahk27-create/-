# Amerith's Curse 👻

## 📖 Overview
*"Where shadows dance and the night never ends..."*

Amerith's Curse transforms your world into a darker, more sinister realm filled with supernatural creatures and eldritch horrors. Experience an endless night where haunted mansions dot the landscape and powerful supernatural entities roam the darkness.

---

## 🎯 Core Features

### Creatures & Mobs 💀
- **Ghosts** - Ethereal entities that phase through walls; vulnerable to special weapons
- **Vampires** - Nocturnal predators with increased strength at night; weak to sunlight potions
- **Reanimated Zombies** - Reskinned with a more menacing appearance
- **Pumpkin Golems** - Sentient guardians of haunted pumpkin patches
- **Spectral Variants** - Enhanced versions of vanilla mobs with ghostly properties

### World Mechanics 🌙
- **Always Night Mode** - Sun never rises; stars shine eternally
- **Mystical Mist** - Atmospheric fog that obscures vision and spawns creatures
- **Haunted Structures** - Procedurally generated mansions with treasure and traps
- **Ethereal Dimension** - Twilight-touched areas with altered physics

### Combat & Weapons ⚔️
- **Monster Slayer Sword** - Purpose-built for hunting supernatural creatures; gains strength with each defeat
- **Ghost Catcher** - Specialized tool for trapping spectral entities
- **Revolver** - Grass-reskinned musket weapon; rapid-fire capability
- **Spirit Bow** - Shoots arrows that ignore armor

### Resources & Materials 💎
- **Amerith Ore** - Rare supernatural mineral found deep underground and in haunted structures
  - *Properties:* Glows faintly in darkness; conducts ethereal energy
  - *Crafting:* Combine with other materials for powerful enchantments
  - *Uses:* Monster Slayer Sword, Ghost Catcher, protective amulets

### Enchantments & Magic 🔮
- **Specter's Blessing** - Allows seeing invisible entities
- **Unholy Pact** - Temporary vampire-like abilities
- **Ghost Warding** - Protection from spectral damage
- **Eternal Night** - Extends darkness effects

### Biomes & Structures 🏚️
- **Haunted Mansion Biome** - Grand estates with multiple rooms, treasure, and boss encounters
- **Cursed Forest** - Twisted trees and ghostly phenomena
- **Spectral Crypt** - Underground burial grounds with rare Amerith deposits
- **Phantom Village** - Abandoned settlements filled with undead

---

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Atmosphere
- Dark purples, blacks, and silver color palette
- Eerie ambient sound design
- Particle effects: ghostly wisps, dark mist, ethereal particles
- Custom textures with supernatural themes
- Haunting soundtrack with orchestral elements

---

## 📊 Progression Path
1. Gather resources and craft basic protection
2. Hunt common undead for materials
3. Locate Amerith ore deposits
4. Craft Monster Slayer Sword
5. Build power to face stronger supernatural entities
6. Access Haunted Mansion boss fights
7. Unlock end-game Amerith weapons

# Maple Forge 🪓

## 📖 Overview
*"In the heart of nature, craft legendary weapons..."*

Maple Forge celebrates craftsmanship and wilderness exploration. A new biome filled with majestic maple forests, towering mountains, and rushing rivers awaits those seeking to master the forge and create custom weapons from rare materials.

---

## 🎯 Core Features

### Biomes & Terrain 🌲
- **Maple Biome** - Expansive forests with golden-red foliage
  - Unique atmospheric lighting and weather
  - Peaceful ambiance with rustling leaves
  - Mushroom circles and natural formations
- **Maple Forest Variants** - Dense woods, sparse groves, enchanted groves
- **River Biome/Ravine** - Flowing waterways with fishing opportunities
  - Cascading waterfalls
  - Riverside cliffs and caves
  - Water-based mob spawning
- **Mountain Biome (Enhanced)** - Towering peaks with dramatic vistas
  - Multiple height layers with unique resources
  - Snow-capped summits
  - Hidden caverns and rare ore deposits
- **Wetland Areas** - Swampy regions with unique flora

### Wood & Vegetation 🌳
- **Maple Wood/Logs** - Primary building material with warm orange-brown hue
- **Maple Leaves** - Seasonal color variations (red, orange, gold)
- **Maple Saplings** - Renewable resource for tree farming
- **Maple Sap** - Resource for crafting adhesives and potions
- **Flowering Bushes** - Decorative and medicinal plants
- **Mushroom Varieties** - Unique fungi in maple groves

### Crafting & Weapons ⚒️
- **Hatchet** - New axe variant optimized for chopping
  - Faster wood collection
  - Lighter weight, faster swing
  - Can fell trees faster
- **Forge (Custom Anvil)** - Advanced crafting station for legendary weapons
  - Requires fuel (charcoal, coal, or special fuel)
  - Multi-step crafting process
  - Visual crafting effects (sparks, heat waves)
  - Creates truly unique items

### Forge Mechanics ⚙️
- **Resource Gathering** - Mine ore, chop wood, collect special materials
- **Smelting** - Refine raw materials into ingots and bars
- **Combining** - Merge materials to create weapon bases
- **Enchanting** - Apply magical properties to weapons
- **Finishing** - Final touches for legendary quality
- **Quality Tiers** - Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary

### Weapons & Tools Created 🗡️
- **Maple Axes** - Standard chopping tools with decorative handles
- **Claymore** - Two-handed sword with Amerith-enhanced blade
- **Hunting Bow** - Specialized for creature hunting
- **Spear** - Polearm weapon with extended reach
- **War Hammer** - Heavy blunt weapon for power
- **Scythes** - For harvesting crops or combat
- **Daggers** - Quick, precision weapons
- **Staves** - Magical weapons for spellcasting

### New Mobs & Creatures 🦅
- **Birds** - Multiple species with unique behaviors
  - Eagles (predatory, majestic)
  - Owls (nocturnal hunters)
  - Ravens (intelligent scavengers)
  - Songbirds (ambient creatures)
- **Bears** - Powerful forest inhabitants
  - Territorial behavior
  - Can be aggravated by logging
  - Drop valuable pelts
  - Multiple species: Brown, Black, Polar variations
- **New Fish Species** - Enhanced fishing mechanics
  - Trout (river fish)
  - Pike (predatory fish)
  - Salmon (migratory)
  - Bass (common catch)
  - Rare exotic species in deep caves

### Tools & Utilities 🔨
- **Saw** - Cuts logs into planks and specific shapes
- **Saw Dust** - Byproduct used for crafting and fuel
- **Forge Hammer** - Special tool for smithing
- **Fishing Rod** - Enhanced with new enchantments
- **Mining Pickaxe (Maple Enhanced)** - Specialized for mountain ore
- **Grinding Stone** - Processes raw materials

### Processing & Refinement ⚗️
- **Sawmill** - Automated wood processing
- **Furnace Networks** - Chain multiple smelters for efficiency
- **Lumber Mill** - Large-scale wood production
- **Grinding Stones** - Pulverize materials into fine powder
- **Mixing Tables** - Combine materials for special properties

---

## 🌲 Resource Management

### Primary Resources
- **Maple Logs** - Common; endless supply through farming
- **Mountain Ore** - Mid-tier; found in mountains
- **River Gems** - Found near water; beautiful decorative stones
- **Beast Pelts** - Dropped by bears; used for crafting
- **Enchanted Flora** - Rare plants with magical properties

### Rare Materials
- **Crimson Sap** - From ancient trees; boosts weapon power
- **Moonstone Dust** - Found in high mountains; adds magic
- **Heartwood** - Core of century-old trees; legendary weapon material

---

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Atmosphere
- Warm autumn color palette (golds, oranges, crimsons)
- Peaceful, contemplative atmosphere
- Ambient sounds: wind, rustling leaves, flowing water, birdsong
- Craftsman-focused aesthetic
- Rustic, cozy settlements

2. Gather basic maple wood and resources
3. Build a simple forge
4. Craft basic tools (hatchet, saw)
5. Explore mountains to find rare ores
6. Defeat wildlife for special materials
7. Unlock advanced forge recipes
8. Create epic and legendary weapons
9. Master all crafting techniques for end-game gear
